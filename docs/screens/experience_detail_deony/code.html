Quiet Morning at the Coast

star star star star star_half
4.5 / 5
location_on Carmel-by-the-Sea, California
schedule 06:30 AM - 09:00 AM

The fog was dense this morning, rolling in off the Pacific with a quiet intensity. It muffled the sounds of the crashing waves, reducing the world to a soft, grey gradient. I sat on the damp sand for over an hour, just watching the edge of the water disappear into the mist.

There is something incredibly grounding about being enveloped in that kind of atmosphere. It strips away distractions. My mind, usually buzzing with the demands of the week, finally settled. I realized I haven't allowed myself to just 'be' in months.

"In the quiet, the loudest thoughts finally have room to dissipate."

I need to remember this feeling. The necessity of emptiness. The archive here is mostly full of doing, but perhaps I need to start documenting the pauses. The spaces between the milestones.
